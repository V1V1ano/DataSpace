# Planetentemperaturen
source mean_temperature and diameter: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/

For it's important to distinguish gas and terrestrial planets.
Mean temperature for rocky planets is the average temperature over the whole surface.
Mean temperature for gas planets is the average temperature at 1-bar pressure level in the atmosphere = earth's atmospheric pressure at approx. sea level
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/solar-system-temperatures/

### Infos und Datenquelle Rocky Planets

min and max temperatures depend on a combined influence of factors:
- distance from the sun (the closer the higher the sun rays influence)
- rotation time of the planet (long rotation = länger Sonnenlicht ausgesetzt = heißer)
- composition + density of planet atmosphere: dense atmosphere have little variation as it moderates heat gain/loss
- axis tilt: high tilts cause big variations in length of days and nights (continous daytime ...)
- orbital eccentrictiy: the more eliptical the bigger the variations
